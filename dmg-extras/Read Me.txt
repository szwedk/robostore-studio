RoboStore Studio — opening it the first time on macOS
======================================================

The app isn't code-signed yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

EASIEST (one click)
  1. Drag "RoboStore Studio" onto the Applications folder.
  2. Double-click  "Open RoboStore Studio.command"  in this window.
     (It clears the flag and opens the app for you.)

OR DO IT MANUALLY
  1. Drag the app to Applications.
  2. Right-click the app -> Open  (then click Open in the dialog).
  3. If macOS says the app is "damaged", open Terminal and paste:

     xattr -cr "/Applications/RoboStore Studio.app"

     ...then open the app again.

You only do this once. It goes away when the app is code-signed + notarized.
